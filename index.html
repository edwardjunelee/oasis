Water bottle + focus pad

Drink more.
Scroll less.

Oasis is a smart water bottle and a foldable desk pad built for study sessions. The bottle tracks every sip. The pad charges it and locks away your distracting apps until the work is done.

The bottle

Knows how much you've had to drink.

A sensor inside the bottle measures the water level and sends it to the Oasis app, so you don't have to log a single sip. Pick it up and the front display shows how full it is. Set it down and the display turns off to save battery.

  • Level trackingBuilt-in water level sensorEvery refill and every sip is logged to your app.
  • DisplaySmall percentage readout on the frontDiscreet, low-glow, easy to read across a desk.
  • Motion wakeTurns on when the bottle movesStays dark while it sits still, which saves battery.
  • InsulationHigh-performance insulated wallsKeeps cold drinks cold and hot drinks hot through a long session.
  • BuildDurable, lightweight materialLight enough for a backpack and tough enough to survive one.
  • ColoursSix finishesStudy Black, Sage, Walnut, Dusk Lavender, Chalk, Lake Blue.
The Focus Pad

Put your phone down. Literally.

The Focus Pad works like a phone "brick." Set your phone on it and the apps you picked in the Oasis app are blocked until you end the session. You choose which apps count as distractions.

  • Focus modeBlocks the apps you choose while your phone is near the pad
  • Bottle dockBuilt-in holder with a magnetic grip that also charges the bottle
  • MousepadOne half is a smooth mouse surface
  • FoldableFolds flat to carry, or stays under your laptop all day
The Oasis app · subscription

See your day in water.

The app graphs how much you drink through the day and points out patterns, like long gaps or drinking most of your water late at night. It is also where you choose which apps the Focus Pad blocks.

The Focus Pad turns "put your phone away" into something physical: set it down and your chosen apps lock. Pairing it with the bottle means one purchase solves both problems at the desk.

Pricing power

Smart features make Oasis hard to replace

A plain bottle loses buyers the moment a cheaper one shows up. Because Oasis does things other bottles don't, a small price change doesn't scare off most of our customers.

Beating the competition

We win on features, not just looks

Insulated bottles are everywhere, and free tools like Screen Time can limit phone use. No one else puts tracking, a charging dock and phone blocking in one system, and that's what we compete on.
